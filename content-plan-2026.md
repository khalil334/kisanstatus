# KisanStatus.com — 10 Article Recovery Plan (Google Spam Update ke baad)

Site spam update me hit hui hai. Recovery ka rasta ek hi hai: aise articles jo
kisi bhi AI-detection ya spam-pattern check me "template content" na lagein.
Neeche 10 naye topics hain — ye sab site pe abhi cover NAHI hain, isliye
existing articles ke saath keyword overlap ka koi risk nahi hai.

Har article ke liye likha hai: kis reader ke liye hai, search intent kya hai,
aur angle kya hoga (matlab article me aisa kya hoga jo baaki 10 ranking pages
me nahi milta).

---

## Likhne ke STRICT rules (har article pe lagu)

Ye rules isliye hain kyunki Google ka spam update exactly wahi pattern pakadta
hai jo AI-generated content me hota hai. Ek bhi rule toota to article publish
mat karo.

**Word count**
- Har article kam se kam 2300 words. Lekin word count bharne ke liye fluff
  nahi — agar 2300 tak pahunchne ke liye repeat karna pad raha hai to topic me
  aur research karo, padding mat karo.

**Keywords**
- Koi bhi keyword repeat karke mat thooso. Main keyword title me ek baar,
  pehle 100 words me ek baar — bas. Uske baad naturally jaise bolchal me aata
  hai waise hi aaye.
- Har heading me keyword daalna sakht mana hai. Headings aisi ho jaise kisi
  dost ko samjha rahe ho ("Ab asli dikkat yahan aati hai", "Bank wale ye kyun
  puchte hain"), na ki "PM Kisan Yojana 2026 Benefits" type.

**AI-uniform structure — sakht mana**
- Har paragraph ki length alag ho. Kabhi 1 line ka para, kabhi 6 line ka.
  Agar saare paras 3-4 line ke hain to wo AI pattern hai — todo usko.
- Sentence length mix karo. Kabhi 4 shabd. Kabhi ek lambi line jisme comma ke
  saath do-teen baatein ek saath keh di jayein jaise insan bolta hai.
- Har article ka section order ALAG ho. Agar article 1 me pehle "kya hai" phir
  "kaise apply kare" phir "documents" hai, to article 2 kisi kisan ki asli
  problem se shuru ho, article 3 seedha process se. Same skeleton 10 articles
  me repeat hua to sab pakde jayenge.
- Koi FAQs section nahi. Bilkul nahi.
- Koi table nahi.
- Koi "Internal Links" / "Ye bhi padhe" wala forced section nahi. Agar link
  dena hi hai to sentence ke beech me naturally aaye, warna mat do.
- Koi "Conclusion" / "Nishkarsh" / "Antim Shabd" template ending nahi. Article
  wahan khatam ho jahan baat khatam hoti hai — jaise insan chitthi khatam
  karta hai, kabhi ek salah ke saath, kabhi ek warning ke saath.

**AI words/phrases — banned list**
- "aaj ke digital yug me", "is lekh me hum janenge", "comprehensive",
  "delve", "moreover", "furthermore", "in conclusion", "gem", "unlock",
  "seamless", "game-changer", "boost", "elevate" — aur inke Hindi versions
  bhi ("vistar se janenge", "ek nazar dalte hain", "to chaliye shuru karte
  hain"). Ye sab dekhte hi kaat do.
- Har article ki opening alag ho. Kabhi ek sawal se, kabhi ek asli ghatna se,
  kabhi seedha jawab se. "Kya aap jaante hain..." type opening 10 me se ek
  baar bhi nahi.

**Real insaan wala content — must**
- Har article me kam se kam 2-3 first-hand cheezein: asli portal ka asli step
  (button ka naam, page ka naam), asli document ka naam jo CSC wala mangta
  hai, asli problem jo form bharte waqt aati hai (OTP nahi aana, server down,
  naam ki spelling Aadhaar se match na hona).
- Rakam, dates, subsidy percent — sirf wahi likho jo official source pe
  verify ho. Andaza laga ke number likhna mana hai. Jo pata nahi, wahan
  saaf likho "apne block ke krishi vibhag se confirm karein".
- Kahin kahin apni ray do — "mera manna hai pehle bank jaake pata karo, phir
  form bharo" — kyunki asli likhne wala ray deta hai, AI nahi deta.

## Article 1 — Pashu Shed Yojana (MGNREGA se gaay-bhains ka shed)

- **Slug:** `pashu-shed-yojana-mgnrega`
- **Reader:** chhota kisan jiske paas 2-4 pashu hain, pakka shed nahi
- **Search intent:** kitna paisa milta hai, kaise apply hota hai, gram
  panchayat me kya kehna hai
- **Angle:** ye scheme MGNREGA ke through chalti hai aur zyada tar articles
  galat batate hain ki online apply hota hai — asliyat me job card aur gram
  sabha se rasta jaata hai. Yehi sach batao, process gram rozgar sevak se
  shuru karo. Job card na hone wali situation bhi cover karo.

## Article 2 — FPO Kaise Banaye (aur 15 lakh wali baat ka sach)

- **Slug:** `fpo-kaise-banaye-registration`
- **Reader:** kisan jo WhatsApp pe "FPO banao 15 lakh lo" wala message dekh
  chuka hai
- **Search intent:** FPO registration process, kharcha, kitne member chahiye
- **Angle:** 15 lakh seedha account me nahi aata — ye equity grant aur
  management cost ke roop me CBBO ke through milta hai. Ye myth-busting hi
  article ki reedh hai. 10 member wali producer company vs cooperative ka
  farak simple bhasha me.

## Article 3 — Polyhouse Subsidy (NHB/NHM)

- **Slug:** `polyhouse-subsidy-nhb-kaise-le`
- **Reader:** kisan jo sabzi/phool ki kheti me polyhouse lagana chahta hai
- **Search intent:** per square meter lagat, subsidy kitni, apply kahan
- **Angle:** asli lagat ka hisaab (structure + drip + fasal) aur ye kadwa
  sach ki subsidy reimbursement model pe hai — pehle apna paisa lagana
  padta hai. Bank loan + subsidy combo kaise kaam karta hai. State horticulture
  mission ke office jaane wala step detail me.
