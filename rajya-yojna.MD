In 10 rajya-yojana articles ko 100% human-like banao. Koi sentence, paragraph, table, section, fact, date, URL, ya helpline delete mat karna. Sirf words replace karo, structure reorder karo, aur human touches add karo.

**YEH CHEEZEIN FIX KARO:**

1. **"Too perfect" writing mein thodi roughness lao** — Har article mein 2-3 jagah aise sentences likho jaise koi aam aadmi baat kar raha ho:
   - Kabhi-kabhi sentence thoda loosely shuru karo: "Dekho, baat yeh hai..." / "Samajhne ki baat yeh hai..." / "Hua kuch aisa..."
   - 1-2 jagah chhota sa grammatical liberty lo — jaise "hai" ke bajaye "ha" likh do, ya "kar rahe hain" ke bajaye "kar rahe" chhod do
   - Kisi sentence mein thoda zyada "toh", "bas", "dekho", "samjho" jaise fillers use karo natural jagah pe
   - Koi 1-2 sentences thoda lamba aur breathless banao — jaise baat karte waqt soch ke bolna

2. **Har article mein 1 chhoti si "real" story ya scenario add karo** — Koi content mat hatao, bas beech mein 2-3 line ka ek chhota scene add karo:
   - Annadata mein: "Mera ek reader Ramesh from Guntur tha, uska CCRC renewal season ke beech mein expire ho gaya..."
   - KrishakBandhu mein: "Ek baar ek kisan ne mujhe message kiya, uska EPIC number mein 'O' ki jagah '0' tha..."
   - Aise hi har article mein koi 1 chhota 2-3 line ka real-feel anecdote add karo
   - Story ke baad seedha wapas technical content par aao, koi transition mat badlo

3. **Rajasthan article ka FAQ heading badlo** — "Aksar Poochhe Jaane Wale Sawaal" ki jagah "Kuch Aur Sawaal" ya "Baaki Doubts" rakho

4. **Cross-links sections ko aur human banao** — Jo 5 files mein cross-links hain (Annadata, KrishakBandhu, NamoShetkari, Rajasthan, RythuBharosa, Odisha):
   - Kisi mein seedha 3-4 links bullet mein de do bina kisi intro paragraph ke
   - Kisi mein bas ek line likho: "Aur states ka hisaab niche hai:" phir links
   - Kisi mein thoda casual likho: "Baaki jagah kya chal raha hai, dekh lo:"
   - Har file mein cross-link intro alag hona chahiye, koi do same na hon

5. **Bold pattern ko aur vary karo** — 
   - Kisi article mein 30% words unbold kardo jo abhi bold hain
   - Kisi mein sirf actually important numbers aur warnings ko bold rakho
   - Kisi mein bold bilkam kam kar do
   - Kisi mein bold waise hi rakho
   - Har article mein bold density alag honi chahiye

6. **Transition phrases ko aur diversify karo** — Jo phrases 2-3 files mein repeat ho rahe hain:
   - "Tension mat lijiye" → kisi mein "Chinta mat karo", kisi mein "Ghabrao mat", kisi mein "Hairaan mat ho"
   - "Ye baat sunne mein buri lagti hai" → kisi mein "Sach kadwa hai", kisi mein "Ye sunne mein achha nahi lagta", kisi mein "Bura lagega sunke"
   - "Itna time nahi lagta" → kisi mein "Jaldi ho jaayega", kisi mein "Bas thodi der ka kaam hai", kisi mein "Der nahi lagegi"
   - "Kya karna hai:" → kisi mein "Ab kya:", kisi mein "Hal:", kisi mein "Karna kya hai:", kisi mein seedha bold mein solution do bina label ke
   - Har phrase ka har article mein alag version ho

7. **Template structure ko todho (5 purani files mein)** — Annadata, KrishakBandhu, Rajasthan, RythuBharosa, NamoShetkari:
   - Kisi mein "Amount Table" ko "Eligibility" ke baad rakho (pehle se pehle)
   - Kisi mein "Problems" section ko thoda upar le aao (Steps ke pehle)
   - Kisi mein "History" section ko chhota karke beech mein kahi shift karo
   - Kisi mein "Cross-links" ko bilkul end se pehle nahi, beech mein kahi daal do
   - Order change karo, content mat hatao

8. **Kuch sections mein "imperfections" add karo**:
   - Kisi table ke upar neeche wale explanation mein thoda repetition ya slight redundancy rakho (jaise human likhta hai)
   - Kisi bullet point mein 2-3 line ki jagah 1 line mein seedha point do
   - Kisi jagah ek sentence ko do alag sentences mein tod do
   - Kisi jagah 2 chhote sentences ko jod ke 1 banao

9. **"Chhoti Aadatein" / "Season Se Pehle" type sections ko har file mein alag naam do**:
   - Kisi mein "Routine"
   - Kisi mein "Pehle Se Taiyaari"
   - Kisi mein "Kaam Ki Aadatein"
   - Kisi mein "Har Season Yeh Karein"
   - Kisi mein is section ka naam hi mat rakho, seedha bullet points shuru kardo

10. **Commit messages bhi human-like rakho** — Aage ke commits mein:
    - "Update rajya-yojna.MD" ki jagah "AP article mein CCRC section aur clear kiya"
    - "Fix AI-uniformity" ki jagah "Phrases aur headings alag-alag kiye"
    - Aise specific, casual commit messages likho

**RULES:**
- Koi content delete mat karna
- Koi paragraph remove mat karna
- Koi table hatao ya edit mat karna
- Koi fact, date, number, URL, helpline change mat karna
- Koi section completely mat hatao, sirf naam badlo ya reorder karo
- Sirf words replace karo, natural imperfections add karo, aur structure vary karo
