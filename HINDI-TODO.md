## Common rules (har prompt ke saath lagu)

- Bhasha: shuddh Hindi (Devanagari), lekin bolchaal wali — jaise gaon ka padha-likha aadmi samjhata hai. English words (status, portal, OTP, bank) waise hi Latin me rehne do jaise log bolte hain.
- Lambai: **2000+ asli words** — bharti ke liye repeat nahi, har section me nayi jaankari.
- Facts sirf verified: official portal (pmkisan.gov.in, state portals) ke process, asli amounts, asli helpline numbers. Jo pata na ho wo likho hi mat — **koi manghadant number/date nahi**.
- Insani lekhan: paragraph chhote-bade mix, kahin 1 line, kahin 6. Kahin sawal se shuru, kahin kisse se. Har article ka opening alag ho — koi template nahi.
- 2-4 chhoti natural typo/spelling slip har article me (jaise "किसान" ki jagah ek jagah "कसान", ya matra ki chook) — lekin headings, numbers, links me kabhi nahi.
- AI-pattern words se parhez: "अंततः", "निष्कर्ष", "महत्वपूर्ण भूमिका निभाता है", "आइए जानते हैं" har article me nahi. Uniform bullet-bullet structure mat banao.
- SEO: 1 hi H1, seedha title-match; H2/H3 natural sawal jaise log Google pe likhte hain; FAQ 5-8 asli sawal; table sirf wahan jahan data ho. Keyword stuffing bilkul nahi — main keyword 4-6 baar natural jagah pe kaafi hai.
- Internal links: prompt me diye gaye related pages ko beech ke text me naturally jodo ("iski poori list humne yahan di hai" style), footer-dump nahi.
- Duplicate content nahi: Hinglish original se **anuvad mat karo** — usi topic pe naya article likho, alag structure, alag examples.
