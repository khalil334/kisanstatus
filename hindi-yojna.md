In articles ko edit karo lekin sirf specific AI uniformity issues fix karo. Koi paragraph delete nahi karna, koi sentence remove nahi karna, sirf words/phrases replace karna hai jahan bata raha hoon.

**KAHAAN KAHAAN FIX KARNA HAI:**

1. **Article ke shuru ke 2-3 sentences mein** — har article ka opening hook alag hona chahiye. Jahan "Gaon mein kist ke dinon ka ek apna hi mahaul hota hai" ya "Log ise ek scheme samajhte hain" jaisi generic line hai, uski jagah us state ki koi specific baat se shuru karo. Sirf pehle paragraph ke pehle 2-3 sentences change karo, baaki waisa hi rakho.

2. **"Ghabraiye mat" phrase** — jahan bhi yeh phrase hai (har article mein 2-3 baar aata hai), uski jagah alag-alag alternatives use karo:
   - "Tension mat lo"
   - "Darne ki zaroorat nahi"
   - "Hairaan hone ki baat nahi"
   - "Chinta chhodo"
   Har article mein alag alternative ho.

3. **"Kadwa sach" phrase** — jahan bhi hai, replace karo:
   - "Ye baat sunne mein buri lagti hai"
   - "Sach mano toh ye thoda tough hai"
   - "Ye baat achhi nahi hai lekin"

4. **"Paanch minute ka kaam" / "Do minute ka kaam"** — har article mein yeh same timing phrase hai. Replace karo:
   - "Jaldi ho jaayega"
   - "Itna time nahi lagta"
   - "Bas thodi der ka kaam hai"

5. **"Ye sabse aam shikayat hai" / "Yahi sabse zyada poocha jaata hai"** — har article mein same transition sentence hai problems section mein. Replace karo:
   - "Log sabse zyada yahi poochhte hain"
   - "Sabse zyada confusion isi baat pe hota hai"
   - "Aksar log yahi kehte hain"

6. **Table ke upar wala sentence** — har table ke upar "Neeche diya table..." ya "Yeh table batata hai..." jaisa same intro hota hai. Table ke upar sirf seedha bold heading rakho, ya ek short line likho jaise "Hisaab kuch aisa banta hai:" — explanation hatao.

7. **Cross-links section (article ke end se pehle)** — jahan "Dusre Rajya Se Hain?" / "Bengal Se Bahar Ke Kisan" / "AP Se Bahar Ke Kisan" wala section hai, uska intro sentence har article mein alag hona chahiye. Kisi mein seedha "Aur states ka hisaab:" likho, kisi mein "Baaki jagah kya chal raha hai:", kisi mein "Doosre states mein:".

8. **FAQ section ka header** — har article mein "Aksar Poochhe Jaane Wale Sawaal" same hai. Har article mein alag heading rakho:
   - "Kuch aur sawaal"
   - "Jo log aksar poochhte hain"
   - "Common doubts"
   - "Aur kuch jaanna hai?"

9. **"Samajh lein" / "Samajhna zaroori hai"** — har article mein 4-5 baar aata hai. Har occurrence ko alag word se replace karo:
   - "Dekh lo"
   - "Jaan lo"
   - "Note kar lo"
   - "Baat yeh hai"

10. **Bold text ka pattern** — har article mein same words bold hain (jaise **Eligible**, **Rejected**, **Solution**). Kuch jagah bold hata do, sirf actually important words ko bold rakho. Har article mein bold words ka pattern alag hona chahiye.

**RULES:**
- Koi sentence structure change mat karna, sirf words replace karna hai
- Koi paragraph remove mat karna
- Koi table remove ya edit mat karna
- Koi fact, date, number, URL, helpline change mat karna
- Koi section heading completely mat hatao, sirf wording change karo
- Har article mein alag-alag replacements use karo, same replacement har jagah mat lagao
