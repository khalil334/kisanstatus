# 10 Articles Plan — Traffic Growth (kisanstatus.com)

> **Research date:** 3 August 2026
> **Kaam kaise hua:** Deep research (web) + repo audit (`lib/articles-data.ts`, `lib/maandhan-data.ts`, `components/articles/`) — taake koi duplicate article na bane.
> **Zaroori baat:** Site ka Ahrefs health score **100/100** hai (`CLAUDE.md` dekho). Yaani clicks kam hone ki wajah **technical SEO nahi** hai — wajah ye hai ke jo 50 articles maujood hain, wo **ek hi topic cluster (PM Kisan status + eKYC)** ke aas paas hain, aur us cluster mein competition sabse zyada hai. Growth in 10 articles se aayegi jo **naye demand pockets** pakadte hain.

---

## Pehle ye samajh lo — 100x kyun mushkil hai, aur kya realistic hai

Main aap se seedhi baat karunga, jhooti ummeed nahi dunga:

**"100x traffic" ek single article se nahi aata.** Is niche (sarkari yojana) mein 100x tab hota hai jab teen cheezein saath chalein:

1. **Naye keyword clusters** — sirf "PM Kisan status" pe 30+ article likhne se aapas mein cannibalization hoti hai (aap ke apne pages ek doosre se lad rahe hain). Neeche wale 10 articles jaan bujh kar **alag-alag clusters** hain.
2. **Timing (sabse bada lever)** — is niche mein 80% traffic **installment release ke 48 ghante** mein aata hai. PM Kisan 23vi kist **20 June 2026** ko release hui thi (9.44 crore kisan, ₹18,880 crore). **24vi kist October 2026 mein expected hai** — yaani aap ke paas **~2 mahine** hain taiyari ke liye. Ye window miss hui to next chance February 2027.
3. **Sabr** — nayi articles ko rank hone mein 3-8 hafte lagte hain. October ki kist se pehle publish karna zaroori hai, warna Google ko trust banane ka waqt nahi milega.

**Realistic target:** agar ye 10 articles October window se pehle live ho jaayein, to **3-5x growth 6 mahine mein** achievable hai. 100x tab jab ye 10 kaamyab hon aur aap isi pattern pe 50-100 articles scale karein.

## Char cheezein jo articles se bhi zyada matter karti hain

1. **Cannibalization theek karo.** 30 PM Kisan articles ek doosre se lad rahe hain. #8 ko hub banao, sab articles usko link karein, aur wo sabko link kare. Jo 2-3 articles bilkul same keyword pe hain unko merge kar do.

2. **Har article mein date stamp.** "Last updated: 3 August 2026" — visible, top pe. Is niche mein Google freshness ko sabse zyada weight deta hai. Aap ke paas `scripts/update-article-dates.js` already hai.

3. **State-wise scale karo (asli 100x).** #1, #3, #4, #5 ka pattern lo aur **ek template component** banao, phir 12-15 states ke liye pages generate karo. Ek article ki mehnat, 15 pages ka traffic. Aap ka Next.js setup iske liye perfect hai (dynamic route + data file).

4. **Speed.** `CLAUDE.md` mein 4 slow pages pending hain (`/calculator/installment-tracker`, `maandhan-pension-card-download`, `mushroom-kheti-nhb-subsidy`, `PmKisanMobileNumberChangeUpdate`). Rural India 4G pe hai — slow page = bounce = ranking down. Server-component refactor pending hai.
