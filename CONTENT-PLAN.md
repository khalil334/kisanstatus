## Asli diagnosis (jo ab data se saabit hai)

Live site pe **50 articles** hain aur quality achhi hai — headings dekh kar lagta hai ground reality se likhe gaye hain ("Documents — Official Vs Actual", "Field Se Aaye Hue Real Sawal", "Private Company Se Mat Lagwana"). Ye normal AI-generated slop nahi hai. Technical health bhi 100/100.

**To phir clicks kyun kam hain? Do wajah:**

### 1. Central government content saturated hai
Aap ke 50 mein se **~30 PM Kisan** + **13 Maandhan** = 43 articles ek hi central-scheme family pe. Ye wo jagah hai jahan **sabse zyada competition** hai — ET Now, Times Bull, BankBazaar, aur 500 chhote sarkari-yojana sites sab yahi likh rahe hain. Aap ka content unse behtar ho sakta hai, lekin unki domain authority zyada hai.

### 2. State schemes pe aap ka **zero** content hai — aur wahan competition kam hai
Live sitemap mein **ek bhi state scheme article nahi** hai. Jabke:

- State schemes PM Kisan se **zyada paisa** dete hain (AP ₹20,000/saal, Telangana ₹12,000/acre)
- Inki search demand **local aur focused** hoti hai — competition central se kam
- Aur sabse ahem: **aap ka existing content inko support karta hai**. Har state scheme PM Kisan beneficiary hone pe depend karti hai — yaani aap ke 30 PM Kisan articles perfect internal-link network ban jaate hain

**Ye plan poora us gap pe hai.**

## Char structural cheezein (articles se zyada important)

### 1. Cannibalization — 43 articles ek family pe
`PmKisanMasterGuide2026` already hub jaisa hai (registration + eKYC + status + payment fail + correction + rejected + mobile change — sab ek jagah). Lekin phir **inhi topics pe alag articles bhi hain**. Ye direct competition hai.

**Fix:** Master Guide ko **sirf hub** rakho — har section chhota karke detail article ko link karo ("Poori detail: [eKYC guide]"). Aur har detail article Master Guide ko wapas link kare. Isse Google ko hierarchy samajh aati hai.

### 2. State-wise templating — asli 100x lever
#1-#7 ka structure bilkul same hai: scheme kya hai → kitna paisa → eligibility → status check steps → kist history → problem fix → FAQ. **Ek template component banao**, data ek file mein rakho, phir 15-20 states scale karo. Aap ka Next.js setup iske liye bana hua hai (dynamic route + data file, jaise abhi `articles-data.ts` hai).

Ek article ki mehnat → 20 pages ka traffic. **Yahi 100x ka rasta hai, koi single article nahi.**

### 3. Date stamps
Aap ke paas `scripts/update-article-dates.js` hai. Har state article pe **visible** "Last updated" chahiye — is niche mein Google freshness ko sabse zyada weight deta hai.

### 4. Speed
`CLAUDE.md` mein 4 slow pages pending hain. Rural India 4G pe hai — slow page = bounce. State articles likhte waqt **shuru se hi** server components use karo, warna wahi problem dobara banegi.
