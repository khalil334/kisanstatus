# CONTENT-RULES.md — STRICT WRITING RULES (Har Article Ke Liye LAZMI)

> Ye rules **mandatory** hain. Koi article in rules ko pass kiye baghair publish NAHI hoga.
> Maqsad: har article 100% insaan ka likha lage — no AI pattern, no copy-paste, no keyword stuffing.

---

## RULE 1 — BANNED AI WORDS & PHRASES (istemaal kiya to REJECT)

**English AI-filler (kabhi use nahi karna):**
- ❌ Moreover, Furthermore, Additionally, In conclusion, In today's world, In this article we will, It is important to note, Delve, Comprehensive, Seamless, Robust, Leverage, Utilize, Navigate the process, Unlock, Empower, Streamline, Crucial, Vital, Essential (baar-baar), Various options available, Plays a significant role, Landscape, Realm, Journey, Game-changer, Elevate, Foster, Harness

**Hinglish AI-filler (ye bhi banned):**
- ❌ "Is article mein hum janenge...", "Aaj ke digital yug mein...", "Jaisa ke hum sab jante hain...", "Ummeed hai ye jankari aapke liye faydemand rahi hogi", "Bina kisi deri ke shuru karte hain", "To chaliye jante hain", "Yah ek mahatvapurn yojana hai" (har article mein), "Sarkar dwara chalayi ja rahi" (opening mein)

**Iski jagah kya likhna hai:**
- ✅ Seedhi baat: "Ab asli baat.", "Yahan log galti karte hain.", "Chhota sa point, par zaroori.", "Seedha jawab pehle:", "Ek minute ruko —"

---

## RULE 2 — SENTENCE LENGTH (AI pattern todo)

AI ka pattern: har sentence 15–20 words, ek jaisi rhythm. **Ye BANNED hai.**

**Lazmi mix (har section mein):**
- Kuch sentences 3–6 words ke. ("Bas. Itna hi kaam hai.")
- Kuch 10–15 words ke.
- Kabhi kabhi ek lambi 25+ words wali — par lagataar do lambi kabhi nahi.
- Lagataar 3 sentences same length ke = REJECT, rewrite karo.
- Kabhi sentence fragment bhi chalega. Jaise ye.

## RULE 3 — PARAGRAPH RULES

- ❌ Har paragraph 3-4 lines ka same size — BANNED (ye AI footprint hai).
- ✅ Mix karo: kahin 1 line ka paragraph. Kahin 5-6 lines ka. Kahin sirf ek sawaal.
- ❌ Har paragraph topic-sentence + 2 supporting + conclusion structure — BANNED.
- ✅ Kabhi paragraph beech baat se shuru ho: "Aur haan, ek cheez bhool gaya —"
- Har section mein kam se kam ek paragraph aisa jo reader se seedha baat kare: "Aap soch rahe honge..."

## RULE 4 — NO REPETITIVE KEYWORDS (strict limits)

- Main keyword SIRF 3 jagah: **title, pehla paragraph, ek H2**. Bas.
- Body mein: max **5–7 baar per 1000 words**. Isse zyada = stuffing = REJECT.
- Do H2 headings mein same keyword — BANNED. Headings kaam ki baat batayein, keyword na dohrayein.
- Variations use karo: "PM Kisan status check" → "kist ka paisa aaya ya nahi", "aapka naam list mein hai ya nahi", "is yojana ka payment".
- "2026" har heading mein — BANNED. Sirf jahan saal ki genuinely zaroorat hai.
- Alt-text mein bhi keyword repeat nahi — describe karo image kya dikhati hai.

## RULE 5 — NO COPY-PASTE (zero tolerance)

1. Research karte waqt SIRF facts note karo (raqam, date, portal ke steps, helpline number). Sentences kabhi copy nahi.
2. Source tab **band karo**, phir memory se apne shabdon mein likho.
3. Sirf official portal ke exact button/menu names quote karna allowed hai ("Know Your Status" option) — kisi site ka paragraph kabhi nahi.
4. Publish se pehle: article ki 3 random lambi lines Google mein quotes ("...") laga kar search karo. Exact match mila = rewrite, phir dobara check.
5. Doosri site ka structure bhi copy nahi — unki heading order dekh kar apni headings mat banao.

## RULE 6 — HAR ARTICLE EK DOOSRE SE UNIQUE (repo ke andar bhi)

- Har article mein **kam se kam 1 section aisa** jo sirf USI topic ke liye ban sakta hai — kisi doosre article mein fit ho hi nahi sakta.
- Opening 4 shapes rotate karo — lagataar do articles same opening shape ke NAHI:
  1. Kisan ki real situation se ("Ramesh ne October mein eKYC kiya, phir bhi kist nahi aayi...")
  2. Seedha jawab pehli line mein
  3. Chaunkane wala number/fact
  4. Common galti jo log karte hain
- Section order har article mein alag. FAQ kabhi 3, kabhi 7, kabhi zero. Table kabhi ho, kabhi prose mein numbers.
- Title shapes rotate karo (sawaal / result / warning / number / simple) — ek category mein lagataar 2 same shape BANNED. "`Topic 2026: Hook`" formula ab default NAHI hai.

## RULE 7 — INSAAN WALA TOUCH (har article mein LAZMI, min 3 cheezein)

- [ ] 1 personal observation/experience: "Maine dekha hai CSC wale ₹50 maangte hain, jabki asli fee ₹15 hai."
- [ ] 1 halka opinion: "Mere hisaab se pehli baar wale bande ko CSC hi jana chahiye."
- [ ] 1 warning/shortcut jo sirf experience se aati hai: "Shaam 5 baje ke baad portal slow hota hai — subah try karo."
- [ ] Desi transitions: "ab asli baat", "yahan zyada tar log phas jate hain", "chalo seedha point pe aate hain"
- [ ] Reader se direct baat: "aapke case mein agar...", "aap soch rahe honge ke..."
- [ ] Specific verifiable details: exact fee, exact button ka naam, exact tareekh. Vague filler ("kai options maujood hain") — DELETE.

## RULE 8 — FAQ & SCHEMA

- FAQ ke sawaal wahi hon jo kisan sach mein poochta hai — "What is PM Kisan?" type generic sawaal BANNED agar body mein already covered hai.
- FAQ answers 2-4 lines, seedhe, kabhi kabhi opinion ke saath. Har answer same length — BANNED.
- JSON-LD schema code repeat hona theek hai (wo code hai, content nahi) — par FAQ text har article ka apna.

## RULE 9 — PUBLISH SE PEHLE FINAL CHECKLIST (sab ✅ hon tabhi publish)

- [ ] Rule 1: koi banned word/phrase nahi
- [ ] Rule 2: sentence lengths mixed (3 lagataar same-length nahi)
- [ ] Rule 3: paragraph sizes mixed
- [ ] Rule 4: keyword sirf 3 fixed jagah + max 5-7/1000 words, headings mein repeat nahi
- [ ] Rule 5: 3 lines ka Google quote-test pass (zero exact match)
- [ ] Rule 6: 1 unique section hai + opening/title shape pichhle article se alag
- [ ] Rule 7: kam se kam 3 insaan-wale touches maujood
- [ ] Zor se padh kar dekho — jahan atak jao ya "robot jaisa" lage, wahan rewrite

**Ek bhi ❌ = article publish NAHI hoga. Pehle fix, phir publish.**
